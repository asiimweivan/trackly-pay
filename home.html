Questions, answered.

Is it free to start?

Yes. You can create an account and start on the free plan.

Which devices can I use?

Any phone, tablet or computer with a web browser. There is also an Android app.

Is my business data safe?

You sign in with your own account, and only the people you invite can see your data. Each person only gets the access you give them.

Can I record Mobile Money sales?

Yes. Record cash and Mobile Money payments on every sale.

Who do I ask for help?

Write to us at support@ivanexo.com.
