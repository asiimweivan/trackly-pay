About Trackly

Trackly is a business management app made in Kigali, Rwanda by Ivanexo Ltd.

Quick facts

  • What it is: a web and Android app for sales, stock, customers, expenses, quotations, purchase orders, projects and reports.
  • Who it is for: shops, restaurants, pharmacies, salons, hardware stores, wholesalers, farms and other small and medium businesses in Rwanda and across Africa.
  • Languages: English, Kinyarwanda and French.
  • Payments: records cash and Mobile Money sales. Subscriptions are paid by Mobile Money.
  • Price: free Ubuntu plan. Paid plans: Ibanze RWF 3,000 per month, Iterambere RWF 6,000 per month, Agaciro RWF 10,000 per month, with discounts for yearly payment. Service passes of 7, 14 or 30 days are also available.
  • Company: Ivanexo Ltd, Kacyiru, Gasabo, Kigali, Rwanda. Email: info@ivanexo.com. Support: support@ivanexo.com.
  • Website: trackly.ivanexo.com and www.ivanexo.com.
