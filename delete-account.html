Delete your Trackly account

Applies to the Trackly app (com.trackly.mobile) · Last updated: 19 October 2026

Option 1: delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Trackly and sign in as the business owner.
  2. Go to Settings, then Data & Storage.
  3. Tap Delete My Account, enter your password and confirm.

Option 2: request deletion by email

If you cannot open the app or have lost your phone, send us a request. Include the phone number you registered with and your business name.

Email a deletion request

We will confirm it is really you (for example by replying to the registered email or calling the registered number) and process the request within 7 days.

What happens next

  • Right away: your account is closed. You cannot sign in and your data is hidden. You get an email confirming this.
  • For 30 days: you can change your mind. Ask for recovery by tapping Request recovery on the sign-in screen or by emailing us, and we will restore the account.
  • After 30 days: your account and all business data (products, stock, sales, invoices, customers, expenses, employees, suppliers and photos) are permanently erased and cannot be recovered.
  • Want it erased immediately? Say so in your request and we will erase it right away, without the 30-day wait.

What we keep

Account and transaction records (amounts, dates, reference numbers) are kept for accounting and tax purposes, with the link to your name and account removed where possible. Copies in backups disappear when our hosting providers' normal backup cycle overwrites them. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Staff and agent accounts

Only the business owner's account can be deleted in the app, and that deletes the whole business including its staff access. Staff members who want their own access or data removed can email us at the address above.
