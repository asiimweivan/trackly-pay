Questions from clinic owners

Is Trackly free for a clinic?

Yes. You can create an account and start on the free plan. Paid plans start at RWF 3,000 per month.

Can I record Mobile Money sales?

Yes. Record cash and Mobile Money payments on every sale.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Trackly works on any phone, tablet or computer with a web browser, and there is an Android app.
