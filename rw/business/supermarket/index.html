Icyo Trackly igufasha ku supermarché

Kugurisha

Gurisha mu masegonda make. Amafaranga mu ntoki cyangwa Mobile Money, umukiriya ahabwa inyemezabwishyu.

Ububiko

Menya ibyo ufite, ibigiye gushira n'ibyo ugomba kugura. Ongeramo ibicuruzwa usoma barcode.

Igabanyirizwa

Tegura promosiyo zikora ubwazo igihe ugurisha.

Gurisha vuba ku kabati, umenye ibiri mu bubiko kandi urebe inyungu yawe buri munsi.

Uko utangira

  1. Fungura kontiIyandikishe ukoresheje telefoni cyangwa Google. Bifata iminota mike.
  2. Andika ibyo ugurishaShyiramo ibicuruzwa cyangwa serivisi zawe.
  3. Tangira kugurishaAndika igurisha rya mbere. Ububiko na raporo bihita bihinduka.

Ibiciro

Tangira ku bwoko bwa Ubuntu bw'ubuntu: kugurisha, amatike, abakiriya, ububiko, imenyesha ry'ibigiye gushira, raporo n'abakozi. Ukeneye ibindi? Ibyishyurwa bitangira kuri RWF 3,000 ku kwezi, ukishyura na Mobile Money. Ushobora no kugura iminsi 7, 14 cyangwa 30 kuri serivisi imwe.

Ibibazo bya ba nyiri supermarché

Trackly ni iy'ubuntu ku supermarché?

Yego. Ushobora gufungura konti ugatangira ku bwoko bw'ubuntu. Ibyishyurwa bitangira kuri RWF 3,000 ku kwezi.

Nshobora kwandika ibyishyuwe na Mobile Money?

Yego. Andika amafaranga mu ntoki cyangwa Mobile Money kuri buri gurisha.

Ikora kuri telefoni yanjye?

Yego. Trackly ikora kuri telefoni, tablet cyangwa mudasobwa ifite browser, kandi hari na porogaramu ya Android.
